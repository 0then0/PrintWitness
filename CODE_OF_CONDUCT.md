# Code of conduct

PrintWitness welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels and viewpoints. Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces, including when representing the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work and its evidence; do not attack the person doing it.
- Give constructive feedback, accept corrections and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Use welcoming language and allow others to participate without intimidation.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, private documents or private correspondence without permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, sustained personal attacks and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Technical disagreement and good-faith criticism are welcome.

## Reporting

The maintainer is [@0then0](https://github.com/0then0). Use a private contact method listed on the maintainer's profile when available. If no private method is listed, open an issue titled **Private conduct report: contact requested**, without names, allegations or other sensitive details, and request a private channel. Do not post the report itself publicly. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Report what happened, where it happened and any relevant evidence once a private channel is established. The maintainer will handle reports discreetly, sharing information only as needed to investigate or respond. GitHub's abuse-reporting tools are also available for violations of its platform rules, including reports concerning the maintainer.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, ask participants to stop, restrict participation or block accounts. Responses should reflect severity, impact and repeated behavior; serious threats or harassment may warrant immediate removal. Participants may request clarification or reconsideration through the reporting channel. Retaliation against someone making a good-faith report is unacceptable.
